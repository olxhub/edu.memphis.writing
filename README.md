# Memphis Introductory Writing

Surveys and reflective journals for an introductory writing course at the
University of Memphis. Students explore their writing habits, attitudes, and
processes through Likert-scale surveys and guided journal prompts with
AI-generated feedback.

## Content

- **Writing Survey 1** — attitudes and feelings about writing
- **Writing Survey 2** — follow-up survey
- **Writing Process Journal 1** — frequency of past writing experiences
- **Writing Process Journal 2** — follow-up journal

Each activity is a standalone OLX sequence (`launchable="true"`), living in
the `edu.memphis.writing` namespace.
